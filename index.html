Centre of Indian Language Data (COIL-D)

COIL-D is a consortium-mode funded project led by IIT Patna with MIT Manipal and other national partners. The project develops language resources for Human Language Technology, including standards, guidelines, benchmarks, and resources for Machine Translation and NLP tools.

  • MIT Manipal contributes parallel corpora for Dravidian languages: Kannada, Tamil, Malayalam, and Telugu.
  • The broader language-technology ecosystem connects with open multilingual translation resources such as AI4Bharat's IndicTrans2.
  • COIL-D datasets are also visible through the coild-aikosh collection on Hugging Face.

Co-Principal Investigator Ministry of Education, Govt. of India

AI Centre of Excellence in Healthcare at MAHE

The AI Centre of Excellence in Healthcare at MAHE is part of a Government of India-backed national AI-CoE effort. Phase 2 is aligned with TANUH, the Translational AI for Networked Universal Healthcare Foundation hosted at IISc Bengaluru.

  • MAHE works with partners including KMC Manipal, AIIMS Delhi, IIT Delhi, IISc, and IIT Madras.
  • The programme focuses on clinically useful AI tools for healthcare, including deep-learning models for coronary artery disease.
  • TANUH develops point-of-care AI diagnostics and decision-support tools for high-burden non-communicable diseases.
